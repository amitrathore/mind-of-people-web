# Mind of People

Drive the outcome. Move the people.

The Outcome Machine for societal movements. One platform, two modes:

- **Driver** — powered by **DatacentrIQ**. Wires into the ad accounts, CRM, donor systems, field tools, and surrogate playbooks you already pay for, owns one metric, and drives ROI on every dollar of campaign spend.
- **Predictor** — powered by **SimPopuli**. Agent-based simulation of the electorate. The polling replacement.

First vertical: Campaign Operations.
